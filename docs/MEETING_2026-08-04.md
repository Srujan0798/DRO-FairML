## Honest negatives

1. **LSAC / DP is degenerate, not a clean “DRO loses.”**  
   DRO loses 0/6 at every α (p=1.0), but accuracy is pinned to the constant predictor (~0.902 vs majority 0.9019) and Naive DP freezes at 0.1827 for α≥0.2. Report as diagnostic collapse (radii overweight tiny minority), not as a fairness comparison. Detail: `docs/LSAC_DEGENERACY.md`.

2. **α ≥ 0.3 is below the constant-predictor baseline on Adult & Credit.**  
   Majority-class accuracies: Adult **0.752**, Credit **0.779**, LSAC **0.902**.  
   From canonical means (DP attack): Adult Naive acc ≈ 0.667 (α=0.3), 0.551 (α=0.4); Credit ≈ 0.753 / 0.751. Comparing two models both worse than “always predict majority” is not a usable robustness claim. **Defensible claim scope: α ≤ 0.2.**
