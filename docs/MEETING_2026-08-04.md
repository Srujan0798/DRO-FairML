## Honest negatives

1. **LSAC / DP is degenerate, not a clean “DRO loses.”**  
   DRO loses 0/6 at every α (p=1.0), but accuracy is pinned to the constant predictor (~0.902 vs majority ~0.902) and Naive DP freezes at 0.1827 for α≥0.2. Report as diagnostic collapse (radii overweight tiny minority), not as a fairness comparison. Detail: `docs/LSAC_DEGENERACY.md`.

2. **α ≥ 0.3 is below the constant-predictor baseline on Adult & Credit.**  
   Majority-class accuracies: Adult **0.752**, Credit **0.779**, LSAC **0.902**.  
   From canonical means (DP attack): Adult Naive acc ≈ 0.667 (α=0.3), 0.551 (α=0.4); Credit ≈ 0.753 / 0.751. Comparing two models both worse than “always predict majority” is not a usable robustness claim. **Defensible claim scope: α ≤ 0.2.**

3. **Adult α≥0.3 under IF attack: DP losses / non-significance.**  
   At α=0.3, DRO **worse** DP than Naive (1/6 wins, p=0.8906) while IF is better (6/6). At α=0.4 DP is n.s. Do not claim “DRO wins all metrics on all attacks at high α.”
