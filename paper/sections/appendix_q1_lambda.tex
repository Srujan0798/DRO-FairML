\subsection{Q1: Lambda Hyperparameter Ablation}
\label{app:lambda-ablation}

In this section, we provide details on the sensitivity of our DRO-FairML framework to the dual variable $\lambda$ initialization and its learning rate $\eta_\lambda$. 

\paragraph{Experimental Setup}
We conducted an extensive grid search over the following parameters:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\lambda_{init} \in \{0.0, 0.01, 0.1\}$
    \item $\eta_\lambda \in \{0.001, 0.005\}$
    \item Random Seeds: 3 distinct initializations
    \item Corruption Levels: $\alpha \in \{0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4\}$
\end{itemize}
This resulted in a total of $3 \times 2 \times 3 \times 4 = 72$ unique configurations. All experiments were performed on the Adult dataset with $\tau=1$ and an inner iteration count of $K_{inner}=10$.

\paragraph{Key Findings}
Our results indicate that tuning the $\lambda$ parameters is beneficial for maintaining Demographic Parity at moderate corruption levels ($\alpha \le 0.2$). However, for higher corruption levels ($\alpha \ge 0.3$), the model's accuracy falls below the $0.752$ constant-predictor ceiling (the accuracy of predicting the majority class), regardless of the $\lambda$ configuration. This suggests that the DRO constraint becomes too restrictive when the corruption budget exceeds 30\%.

\paragraph{Recommendation}
Based on this ablation, we recommend the default settings of $\lambda_{init}=0.0$ and $\eta_\lambda=5\times 10^{-3}$ for general use, as they consistently provided stable convergence across different seeds.
