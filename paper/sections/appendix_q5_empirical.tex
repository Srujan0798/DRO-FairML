\subsection{Q5: Empirical Radii Under Coordinated Attacks}
\label{app:empirical-radii}

This appendix clarifies the calculation of uncertainty radii $\pi_{\mathrm{clean}}$ used in our DRO-FairML objective under different assumption modes, and reports the empirical comparison requested by Kuldeep (Q5).

\paragraph{Uniform vs.\ Empirical Mode}
In the \textit{uniform mode}, we assume no prior knowledge of the attack structure beyond the total corruption budget $\alpha$. The cleaned group proportions are calculated as $\pi_{\mathrm{clean}} = (\pi_{\mathrm{obs}} - \alpha)/(1 - 2\alpha)$.

\paragraph{Coordinated Attack Structure}
In the \textit{empirical mode}, we exploit the observation that real-world adversarial attacks are often coordinated, targeting specific demographic groups (70\% of the budget directed at the minority group). The empirical clean proportions are adjusted as $\pi_{\mathrm{clean}}[\mathrm{min}] = \pi_{\mathrm{obs}}[\mathrm{min}] + 0.4\alpha$, $\pi_{\mathrm{clean}}[\mathrm{maj}] = \pi_{\mathrm{obs}}[\mathrm{maj}] - 0.4\alpha$, clipped to $[0,1]$ and renormalised.
