\subsection{Methods, Attacks, Hyperparameters}
We compare Naive-FAIR (fixed Lagrange multiplier) vs.\ DRO-FAIR
(adaptive worst-case reweighting within a TV uncertainty set).
Attacks: DP-only, IF-only, Combined, with $\alpha\in\{0.0, 0.1, 0.2,
0.3, 0.4\}$.  The tabular $\tau{=}1$, $k_{\mathrm{inner}}{=}10$
grid is complete (540 rows: 3 datasets $\times$ 5 $\alpha$ $\times$ 3
attacks $\times$ 6 seeds $\times$ 2 methods) and achieves Wilcoxon
$p{<}0.05$ at $\alpha\le0.2$ on Adult and Credit under the DP-targeted and
Combined attacks (Adult/DP $\alpha{=}0.1$ is $5/6$).  UTKFace uses the same
protocol on real image features (90 rows; \S\ref{sec:results-utkface}).

Prediction temperature is fixed at $\tau{=}1$ across all
$\alpha$ (per Kuldeep's Q12).  The earlier $\tau{=}100$ schedule for
$\alpha\le 0.3$ is the artifact behind the previous ``DRO is
fragile'' finding.  Inner-loop PGD: $K_{\mathrm{inner}}{=}10$,
$\mathrm{pgd\_steps}{=}20$.  Dual ascent: $\eta_{\lambda}{=}5\!\times\!10^{-3}$,
$\lambda_{\max}{=}1.5$, $\lambda$ initialised at $0.0$.
Algorithm-1 step order is $\theta\!\to\!\lambda\!\to\!\tilde p$ and the
inner max ascends $\nabla g$ (not $\lambda\nabla g$).

Statistical significance uses Wilcoxon signed-rank test on per-seed
DP (or IF) deltas.  No oracle information (true per-group corruption
rates / mask) is ever passed to the trainer.

\textbf{Augmented-Lagrangian variant.}
The trainer optionally adds a quadratic penalty
$\tfrac{\mu}{2}g_{\mathrm{dp}}^{2}+\tfrac{\mu}{2}g_{\mathrm{if}}^{2}$ to
the Lagrangian (a penalty-only augmented Lagrangian; see
\S\ref{sec:results-al}), controlled by \texttt{aug\_lagrangian\_mu}.
$\mu{=}0$ recovers the canonical objective
\emph{exactly} --- verified bit-identical against the locked canonical
rows on a full end-to-end run --- so \emph{every} result reported in
this paper's canonical tables is obtained at $\mu{=}0$ and is
unaffected by the AL variant.  The $\mu{>}0$ experiments are reported
separately in \S\ref{sec:results-al}.
