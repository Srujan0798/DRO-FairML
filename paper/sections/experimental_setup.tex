\subsection{Methods, Attacks, Hyperparameters}
We compare Naive-FAIR (fixed Lagrange multiplier) vs.\ DRO-FAIR
(adaptive worst-case reweighting within a TV uncertainty set).
Attacks: DP-only, IF-only, Combined, with $\alpha\in\{0.0, 0.1, 0.2,
0.3, 0.4\}$ (tabular) and $\alpha\in\{0.0, 0.1, 0.2\}$ (UTKFace, when
unblocked).  3 seeds per cell in this paper; 3 additional seeds
($n{=}6$) are running to enable Wilcoxon $p<0.05$ (the minimum
achievable $p$ with $n{=}3$ is $0.125$).

Prediction temperature is fixed at $\tau{=}1$ across all
$\alpha$ (per Kuldeep's Q12).  The earlier $\tau{=}100$ schedule for
$\alpha\le 0.3$ is the artifact behind the previous ``DRO is
fragile'' finding.  Inner-loop PGD: $K_{\mathrm{inner}}{=}10$,
$\mathrm{pgd\_steps}{=}20$.  Dual ascent: $\eta_{\lambda}{=}5\!\times\!10^{-3}$,
$\lambda_{\max}{=}1.5$, $\lambda$ initialised at $0.0$.
Algorithm-1 step order is $\theta\!\to\!\lambda\!\to\!\tilde p$ and the
inner max ascends $\nabla g$ (not $\lambda\nabla g$).

Statistical significance uses Wilcoxon signed-rank test on per-seed
DP (or IF) deltas.  No oracle information (true per-group corruption
rates / mask) is ever passed to the trainer.
